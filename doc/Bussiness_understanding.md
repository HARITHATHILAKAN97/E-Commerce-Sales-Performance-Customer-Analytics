# Project 1 — Business Understanding

## Project
**E-Commerce Sales Performance & Customer Analytics**

**Domain: E-Commerce / Marketplace  
**Dataset: Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Dataset

## 1. Business Problem

The marketplace needs to understand its sales performance, customer behavior, product/category performance, seller performance, delivery operations, and customer satisfaction in order to identify opportunities for revenue growth and operational improvement.

## 2. Business Objective

Use data to identify:

- Revenue and sales trends
- High-performing products and categories
- Valuable customer segments
- Strong and weak sellers
- Delivery and logistics issues
- Customer satisfaction patterns
- Geographic opportunities

The final goal is to turn these findings into clear business recommendations.

## 3. Key Business Questions

### Sales
- What are the total sales, orders, and average order value?
- How are sales changing over time?
- Which categories generate the most revenue and orders?

### Customers
- Who are the highest-value customers?
- How many customers are repeat customers?
- What customer segments can be identified?
- Which regions have the highest customer demand?

### Products & Sellers
- Which product categories perform best?
- Which sellers generate the most sales and orders?
- Which sellers or categories need attention?

### Delivery & Customer Experience
- What is the average delivery time?
- What percentage of orders are delivered late?
- Does late delivery relate to lower review scores?
- Which categories or regions have delivery problems?

### Payments
- Which payment methods are most commonly used?
- What is the average transaction value by payment method?

## 4. Key KPIs

| KPI | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Total Sales  | Overall business performance |
| Total Orders | Order volume |
| Unique Customers | Customer base |
| Average Order Value | Average transaction value |
| Sales by Category | Category performance |
| Repeat Customer Rate | Customer retention |
| Average Review Score | Customer satisfaction |
| On-Time Delivery Rate | Delivery performance |
| Late Delivery Rate | Logistics problems |
| Average Delivery Days | Delivery speed |
| Freight Cost | Shipping cost |
| Sales by Region | Geographic performance |
| Seller Sales / Orders | Seller performance |


## 5. Central Business Case

**How can Olist improve sustainable marketplace growth by understanding its revenue drivers, customer value, operational performance, and customer experience?**

The analysis will identify key business opportunities, operational problems, and areas requiring management attention.

---

## 6. Analytical Approach

The project will use:

* KPI and trend analysis
* Customer segmentation and RFM
* Cohort analysis
* Product/category and seller benchmarking
* Delivery and geographic analysis
* Pareto analysis
* Correlation analysis
* Hypothesis testing where appropriate
* Root-cause analysis

---

## 7. Scope & Limitations

* Analysis is based on the available historical public Olist dataset.
* Findings represent patterns in the dataset and should not be interpreted as confirmed causal relationships.
* True company profit will not be calculated because a complete cost structure is not available.
* Data-quality issues identified during validation will be investigated and addressed before analysis.
* Final findings will be translated into actionable business recommendations.

## 11. Success Criteria

The project should answer four questions:

**What is happening? **Why is it happening? **Where is the opportunity/problem? **What should the business do next?**

The analysis should connect every major finding to a **business action or recommendation**.
